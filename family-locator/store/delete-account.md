# Delete your Family Locator account

**In the app (fastest):**

- **Parents:** Settings → *Delete my account*. If you're the last parent in the family, the whole
  family is deleted, including every child's location history.
- **Children:** tap ⋮ (top right) → *Delete my data and leave*.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

**Without the app:** email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you use to contact us, with the subject
"Delete my Family Locator data" and your family member name. Because accounts have no email or
password, we verify the request with the family's parent before deleting. We complete requests
within 30 days.

**What is deleted:** profile and name, location and location history, check-ins and alerts,
"who looked" records, tasks, push-notification tokens, and the sign-in itself.

**What may be kept:** nothing tied to you. Aggregate, anonymous service logs are kept for up to 30 days.
