[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") makes Family Locator, an app that lets family members share
location with each other so parents know their children got where they were going safely. This
policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## Who the app is for

A parent or guardian sets up a family and invites their children and other guardians with a
one-time code. The parent is responsible for the child's use of the app and gives consent on the
child's behalf. Children can always see what is shared about them, see when a parent looked, and
pause sharing.

## What we collect

| Data | From | Why | Shared with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Precise location (including in the background) | Child devices, and parents during SOS | Show the child's position to their family, detect arrivals at and departures from saved places, attach location to SOS and check-ins | Only members of the same family |
| Location history | Child devices in "Live location" mode | The day timeline | Family parents. **Deleted automatically after 30 days.** |
| First name / display name | Every member | Show who is who in the family | Family members |
| Saved places (name, position, radius) | Parents | Arrival/departure alerts | Family members |
| Check-in messages, tasks | Members | Family communication | Family members |
| Device status: battery level, whether location/notification permissions are on, app version | Child devices | Warn parents when sharing has stopped working | Family parents |
| A random account identifier and push-notification token | Every device | Sign-in without email or password; delivering alerts | Our service providers only (below) |

We do **not** collect contacts, messages, photos, browsing history, app usage or microphone audio.
We do not use advertising IDs, show ads, sell data, or share data with data brokers or advertisers.

## Service providers

We store data with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Cloud
Functions), operated by Google LLC, in [REGION, e.g. europe-west1]. Map images are loaded from
[MAP PROVIDER, e.g. MapTiler]; requests to it include the map area being viewed but no account information.

## How long we keep data

- Location history, alerts and "who looked" records: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
- Everything else: until the account is deleted.

## Your choices and rights

- **Privacy modes**: a parent can switch a child to "Places only" (no map position or route) or
  "Check-ins only" (nothing until the child checks in or presses SOS).
- **Pause**: a child can pause sharing for a time the parent allows. Parents are told.
- **Delete your account**: in the app, Settings → Delete my account (parents) or
  ⋮ → Delete my data and leave (children). A child's location history, check-ins, alerts and
  profile are deleted immediately. When the last parent deletes their account, the whole family's
  data is deleted. You can also request deletion at [DELETION PAGE URL] or by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL].
- Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights
  to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to processing. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## Children

The app is used by children under the supervision of a parent who sets it up. We collect only the
data listed above, only to provide the service to the family, and never for advertising. Parents
can review or delete their child's data at any time as described above. Where the law requires
verifiable parental consent (for example COPPA in the US), the parent provides it when creating
the family and inviting the child.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access rules ensure only members of a family can
read that family's data.

## Changes

We will post changes here and, for material changes, notify parents in the app.
