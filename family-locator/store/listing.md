## Full description [Play 4000 / Apple 4000]

```
Know your kids got where they were going, without spying on them.

Family Locator shows where your children are, tells you when they arrive at school or get home, and puts their exact location in your hand the moment they press SOS. It's built to be honest with parents and fair to kids.

ALWAYS FREE: SOS, saved places, arrival alerts, route history and "ring loudly". No ads. No pay-per-minute features.

FOR PARENTS
• Live map with honest accuracy: every position shows how precise it is (±m) and how old it is. If a phone goes quiet, you're told, not shown an old dot.
• Places: home, school, practice. Get arrival and departure alerts, with schedules (school alerts on school days only) so you aren't spammed.
• Quiet hours: non-urgent alerts wait for a morning summary. SOS always gets through.
• Tracking-stopped alerts: if location is switched off, permission changes or the phone dies, you'll know why.
• Day timeline: "Home 07:10 → walked 1.2 km → School 08:15–15:30".
• Ring loudly: plays even on silent until they tap "I hear you".
• Live for 5 minutes: fast updates while you're waiting at pickup.
• Tasks & points: reward chores and reading.
• More than one parent or guardian can join.

FOR KIDS
• Big SOS button with a 5-second cancel window for accidental presses.
• One-tap check-ins: "I'm OK", "On my way", "Pick me up please".
• See exactly what your family can see, and when they looked.
• Pause sharing for a while (your parents choose the maximum, and they're told).
• Teen-friendly modes: "Places only" (no map dot, no route) or "Check-ins only".

BATTERY-FRIENDLY
GPS works hard only when it's needed: on the move, during live view or SOS. At home or school it rests.

PRIVACY
• Location is shared only with your family, never sold, never used for ads.
• History is deleted after 30 days.
• No microphone listening, no hidden mode. A notification is always visible while sharing is on.
• Delete your account and data at any time, in the app.

HOW IT WORKS
1. A parent installs Family Locator and creates a family.
2. Settings → Add family member gives a 6-letter code.
3. Install Family Locator on your child's phone, choose "I have a family code" and enter it.

This app monitors the location of children's devices for their parents or guardians, with a visible notification on the child's device. It is not an emergency service: in an emergency call your local emergency number.
```
